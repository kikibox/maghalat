Export sales terms — Abgostar Fara Parsian (AFP)

These terms apply to export orders. The Persian domestic-sales policy is separate. The order-specific pro forma invoice takes precedence over this general page; mandatory applicable law remains unaffected.

Destinations, products and minimum order

Current export destinations are Iraq, Afghanistan, Armenia, Georgia and Tajikistan. Sales to individuals are accepted.

Export products include AFP-manufactured modern irrigation products: side-seam and flat-dripper irrigation tapes, reinforced layflat hoses, unreinforced layflat hoses and polyethylene fittings. Product specifications are stated in the technical sheets and agreed order.

The minimum export order is one full transit-truck load. There are no additional product or quantity restrictions beyond the stated minimum order.

Prices, packaging and taxes

Prices are calculated using current market conditions and fluctuations. A pro forma invoice is valid for 24 hours. The agreed price, settlement currency and order details must be specified in that invoice.

Quoted prices include export packaging and the included product-related costs. Transport from the factory to the destination is paid by the buyer. Customs, insurance and other buyer responsibilities are set out below.

Payment may be made in an agreed foreign currency or Iranian rials (IRR). Transfer charges and currency-conversion costs are borne by the buyer. VAT is added when an official tax invoice is issued, as applicable.

Ordering and payment

Orders are confirmed through the company’s published contact channels, request form or in person. Payment terms may be negotiated for the order; full payment before dispatch is the usual arrangement.

The payment schedule and method are recorded in the pro forma invoice. A payment delay may delay or cancel dispatch.

Collection, loading, transport and customs

The delivery/collection location is the factory gate. Initial loading at the factory is the seller’s responsibility. Transport is the buyer’s responsibility; no named Incoterms rule is automatically implied by this wording.

The buyer selects and coordinates the carrier and transport method. The company provides guidance and cooperation. Freight from the factory, transport insurance, export formalities, import clearance, duties, destination taxes and destination permits are arranged and paid for by the buyer.

The company supplies documents requested for the order; document requirements must be communicated before order confirmation. Any named trade term, precise delivery point and special allocation of responsibilities must be stated in the pro forma invoice.

The buyer is responsible for following up transport delays, loss and non-arrival with the carrier and insurer. This does not remove the seller’s initial-loading or confirmed manufacturing-defect responsibilities.

Preparation and delivery times

Preparation time depends on the order, production demand and the agreement with the buyer; it is specified for each order.

Transport time depends on the destination and is usually about one week, including customs time. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery deadline. The domestic 7–15-working-day rule does not automatically apply to exports.

Inspection and reporting

The buyer must report shortages, visible transport damage and discrepancies within 24 hours after receipt and provide video evidence for review.

Manufacturing defects that become apparent during use may be reported during the warranty period, even if the packaging has been opened and the product used. They are not excluded by the 24-hour rule for apparent issues.

Warranty and confirmed manufacturing defects

The manufacturing-defect warranty lasts one agricultural growing season from the start of cultivation/use in that season. Covered examples include bursting or inadequate water discharge caused by a manufacturing defect. Improper use is not covered.

A company expert must confirm the manufacturing defect in a written inspection report. Replacement, substitute goods, a price adjustment or refund is agreed with the buyer.

For a confirmed manufacturing-defect return, the buyer is responsible for loading the return shipment. The seller bears the other related costs, including inspection, return transport, unloading, replacement and re-dispatch as applicable. Initial loading at the seller’s factory remains the seller’s responsibility.

Returns and cancellation

After dispatch, a change-of-mind return is not accepted. Returns are limited to defects or discrepancies and require the seller’s prior written approval. Apparent discrepancies must be reported within 24 hours of receipt.

Goods returned for an apparent discrepancy must be unused. A confirmed manufacturing-defect claim discovered during use remains eligible under the seasonal warranty.

Approved returns go to the manufacturing factory in Iran, using the return address and method confirmed in writing by the seller. For discrepancies that are not confirmed manufacturing defects, return logistics and cost allocation must be agreed in writing before shipment.

Cancellation is possible before dispatch, except for goods already produced with customer-specific features, such as special weight or a special polyethylene formulation. Any permitted cancellation and related buyer costs must be agreed before goods are moved.

Refunds and deductions

Refunds are settled by agreement between the parties, in the agreed currency and by the agreed method, within a maximum of 20 working days. For an approved return, the refund is processed after the goods have been received and inspected at the factory; the settlement agreement records the start of the refund period.

If tax relating to the order has actually been paid and debited from the company’s account, that amount is taken into account as a deduction in the refund settlement, subject to applicable tax rules. No hypothetical tax deduction is applied.

Initial freight, customs charges and banking or transfer fees are not ordinarily refundable. For a confirmed manufacturing defect, the seller’s specific cost obligations stated above remain applicable.

Support, governing law and exceptional events

Export-order support is available in English through the company’s published contact channels.

The laws of the Islamic Republic of Iran, as the country of manufacture, apply. Disputes are first addressed through negotiation and, if unresolved, through the competent judicial authorities of Isfahan Province.

Transport disruptions, restrictions, permit issues and other events outside the parties’ control are addressed according to the order’s pro forma invoice and applicable law. The buyer coordinates matters within its transport, customs and destination responsibilities with the company.

Privacy and intellectual property

Customer information is kept confidential and disclosed only with consent or as required by law. Marketing messages are sent only with the buyer’s consent.

All rights in the company’s designs, trademarks and logos are reserved. The seller is responsible for supplying goods matching the agreed specifications and addressing confirmed manufacturing defects.
